# Глава 1 «Dusk» — сценарий

«A Winter Night in a Maine Lighthouse, 1892. Part 1: Dusk». Fort Point Light, Stockton Springs, Мэн. Вечер субботы 16 января 1892 года, примерно с 15:30 до 17:30 по стандартному времени.

Черновик для чтения автором. Голос не синтезирован.

## Как читать

- Английский текст — то, что читает диктор. Русское пояснение под каждой сценой — для автора, в озвучку не идёт.
- `{c13}` после фразы — номер утверждения в [списке источников](../research/sources.md#16-список-утверждений-для-главы-1-dusk). `{c8~}` — проверено частично. `{r34}` — реконструкция: в тексте она подана как допущение («perhaps», «we imagine», «likely») или как ощущение, не как факт.
- `[scene: …]` — событие мира для раскадровки. `[pause 3s]` — пауза диктора.
- Фразы без пометки — ощущения, движение, обращение к зрителю. Фактов о прошлом в них нет.
- «Вы» — собирательный смотритель. Реальный смотритель станции, Adelbert G. Webster, не изображается.

Объём текста диктора: 2 178 слов, 11 359 знаков, паузы 4,5 минуты. При 110 словах в минуту — около 24 минут, при 130 — около 21. Точную длину даст синтез; до 25 минут добираем паузами, не словами.

## 1. Вступление

Пояснение: тихий вход. Место, дата, одно дело на вечер — зажечь лампу на закате. Обещание: никаких событий, только работа и ночь.

[scene: lantern flame ident; the flame becomes the lit kitchen window; wide shot of the point at late afternoon]

Part one. Dusk.

[pause 2s]

It is a Saturday in the middle of January, in the year eighteen ninety-two. You are on the coast of Maine, at the place where the Penobscot River opens into the bay. {c1}

[pause 2s]

There is a small light station here, on a point of land called Fort Point. And for this one night, you are its keeper. {c1}

[pause 2s]

You have one task before the day ends. When the sun goes down, the lamp at the top of the tower must be burning. {c13}

[pause 2s]

Nothing else is required of you. There is no hurry. There is only the work, and the evening, and the long winter night that comes after.

[pause 3s]

So let the day settle around you. Let your shoulders drop. We will walk through this evening slowly, one small thing at a time.

[pause 4s]

## 2. Мыс днём

Пояснение: общий план станции. Дом, мастерская, башня — одно здание. Колокольня отдельно. Отель для летних гостей — его закрытость зимой реконструкция.

[scene: slow camera drift across the point; afternoon light, low and pale]

The afternoon light in January is low and thin. It comes across the water at a long angle, and it does not stay long.

[pause 2s]

Look at the station from the edge of the point. There is the keeper's house, a wooden house painted white, with its chimney and its small windows. {c4}

[pause 2s]

And joined to the house, by a narrow room of brick, is the tower. It is not tall, and it is not round. It is square, built of brick and painted white, with the glass lantern on top. {c4}

[pause 2s]

That narrow brick room is the workroom. It means that you can walk from the warm kitchen to the foot of the tower without stepping outside. On a night like this one, that is a gift. {c4~}

[pause 3s]

A light has stood on this point since eighteen thirty-six. The brick tower you are looking at was built in eighteen fifty-seven. So it is already older than you might think, and it has seen many winters like this one. {c6}

[pause 3s]

A little way off, about two hundred and fifty feet to the east and north, stands a second, smaller tower. It is made of wood, shaped like a pyramid, and painted white. It is only two years old. {c7}

[pause 2s]

Inside it hangs the fog bell. When fog comes in over the bay, a machine strikes the bell, once every ten seconds, so that ships can hear the point even when they cannot see it. {c8~}

[pause 3s]

The book of instructions has rules for bell machines like this one. Never leave the machine alone while it is running; someone competent must be in constant attendance upon it. And always take off the winding crank as soon as the machine is wound. {c44}

[pause 3s]

Tonight, we will imagine the air is clear. The bell will likely stay silent. {r39}

[pause 3s]

Near the station there is a large hotel, built in the eighteen seventies for summer guests from the cities. {c12~} In January, we can picture it closed and dark, its long porches empty, its rooms waiting for summer. {r37}

[pause 4s]

## 3. Правило заката

Пояснение: главное правило службы и точное время заката. Часы смотрителя неизвестны — называем время по стандартному поясу и не говорим, по каким часам он жил.

[scene: the sun touching the far shore; long shadows; the tower's west face turning gold-grey]

There is a book of instructions for every keeper in the Light-House Service. It is small and plain, and it is very clear. {c13}

[pause 2s]

It says that lights must be lighted punctually at sunset, and must be kept burning at full intensity until sunrise. {c13}

[pause 3s]

It also says that all preparations must be made early, so that there may be no delay in lighting. {c13}

[pause 3s]

That is why the afternoon matters. Most of the work of a lighthouse is done in daylight, so that the moment of lighting can be calm.

[pause 3s]

Today the sun sets at twenty-three minutes past four, by standard time. Half an hour later, the last of the twilight will be gone. {c30}

[pause 2s]

You do not need to watch the clock. You will see it in the sky. The water darkens first. Then the far shore. Then the sky, slowly, from the east.

[pause 4s]

## 3а. Кто держит свет

Пояснение: кого Light-House Board ставила смотрителем на станции с одним человеком; сады; жалованье этой станции. Был ли «вы» моряком — допущение со словом «perhaps».

[scene: the keeper's house in cut-away; a coat on a peg, a sea chest under the window; the frozen garden plot outside]

Who keeps a light like this one?

[pause 2s]

The Light-House Board wrote that at stations needing only one keeper, it often chose retired sea captains or mates who had families. {c42}

[pause 2s]

So perhaps you have been to sea yourself. Perhaps you know, from the other side, what it means to see a steady light on a dark shore. {r44}

[pause 3s]

The Board also encouraged its keepers to work small farms or gardens, where the land allowed. {c42} In January, whatever garden there is lies asleep under the cold.

[pause 3s]

And the pay for this station, in the government register for this year, is five hundred dollars. {c11}

[pause 4s]

## 4. Лампа греется на кухне

Пояснение: правило о холодной погоде. Заправленную лампу держат в комнате с умеренной температурой, чтобы масло не застыло. В какой комнате — реконструкция.

[scene: cut-away of the house; the kitchen; a brass lamp waiting on the table near the stove]

The lamp is not in the tower yet. It is here in the house, waiting.

[pause 2s]

The book has a rule for very cold weather. When the lamp is filled and ready for the evening, it should be kept in a room of moderate temperature, so that the oil does not chill. {c18}

[pause 3s]

So tonight the lamp sits somewhere warm, not too close to the stove, not too far from it. Let us say it sits on the kitchen table, its brass catching a little of the window light. {r41}

[pause 3s]

It was filled earlier in the day. The rule says to fill it nearly, but not quite, to the top. A little room is left, because the oil grows larger as it warms. {c16}

[pause 3s]

You can rest your hand near it and feel nothing at all. Cold brass. Still oil. A wick waiting.

[pause 4s]

## 5. Масло днём

Пояснение: минеральное масло (керосин), его проверка и правила хранения. Масляного дома в 1892 году ещё нет — где держали масло, реконструкция.

[scene: a closed oil can on a shelf in the workroom; the light through the small window going grey]

The lamp burns mineral oil. Kerosene. By now it is used throughout the lighthouse service. {c29}

[pause 2s]

A book from the Light-House Board, printed in eighteen eighty-nine, compares it with lard oil. Five gallons of mineral oil, it says, give as much light as four gallons of lard oil, and cost far less. {c29}

[pause 3s]

The Board set tests for the oil it would accept. It had to pass a flash test at one hundred and forty degrees, and stay clear and liquid even at zero. {c29}

[pause 3s]

There are rules about oil, and you keep them without thinking. The oil must be drawn only by daylight. Under no circumstances should it be drawn at night. {c21}

[pause 2s]

The cans are kept tightly closed, and the oil room cool and aired. No one smokes there, and no uncovered light is ever taken there. {c20} {c21}

[pause 2s]

And oil is never, ever poured into a lamp while it is lit, or anywhere near a flame. {c20}

[pause 3s]

There is no separate oil house at this station yet. That will come years later. {c10} So for now, we imagine the cans stored somewhere cool and safe, away from the stove. {r38}

[pause 4s]

## 6. Форма и фартук

Пояснение: форма обязательна всегда, кроме чистки и уличной работы. Утром — фартук из льна, чтобы одежда не царапала линзу.

[scene: a peg by the workroom door; a dark coat and a cap; a folded linen apron]

By the workroom door hangs your coat. The service asks its keepers to wear the prescribed uniform at all times. {c28}

[pause 2s]

The cloth is dark blue. The cap carries a small badge of yellow metal, shaped like a lighthouse. {c28~}

[pause 3s]

There are two exceptions. For outdoor work there is a brown working suit. And when you clean the lens, the coat comes off, and a linen apron goes on. {c23} {c28}

[pause 2s]

The apron is not for your sake. It is for the glass. Rough wool could scratch the lens, and linen will not. {c23}

[pause 3s]

This morning, you wore that apron. You cleaned the lens and the glass of the lantern, as you do every day. {c22}

[pause 2s]

First the feather brush, to lift away every grain of dust. Then a soft linen cloth. And last of all, the buff-skin, to polish. {c22}

[pause 3s]

The book warns that a skin which has been wet must never be used, because it will scratch. So the buff-skin is kept dry, folded, in its place. {c22}

[pause 4s]

## 7. Через мастерскую в башню

Пояснение: переход по зданию без выхода на улицу. Устройство лестницы внутри башни неизвестно — не описываем материал и форму.

[scene: camera follows you from the kitchen through the narrow brick workroom; the wall opens into section; the stairs of the tower]

It is nearly time.

[pause 2s]

You put on your coat and your cap. You take the lamp from the table, carefully, with both hands. It is heavier than it looks.

[pause 3s]

You walk through the narrow brick workroom. Your footsteps sound different here. The walls are close. The air is a little colder with every step away from the kitchen. {c4}

[pause 3s]

At the foot of the tower, you begin to climb. The stairs turn upward in the half dark. You do not need a light for them. You know every step.

[pause 4s]

As you climb, the sounds of the house fall away below you. Soon there is only your breath, and the stairs, and the wind against the brick.

[pause 4s]

## 8. Занавески

Пояснение: днём занавески защищают линзу от солнца — линза собирает лучи. Снимают их к вечеру (вторичный источник: «частично»).

[scene: the lantern room; linen curtains hanging around the lens; dim blue light through them]

At the top, you step up into the lantern.

[pause 2s]

All day, linen curtains have hung around the inside of the glass. You hung them this morning, when you put out the light at sunrise. {c24}

[pause 3s]

The reason is simple. A lens that can throw a light sixteen nautical miles across the water can also gather the sun. Left uncovered on a bright day, it could focus the sunlight and do harm, and the glass could slowly change its color. {c24} {c2}

[pause 3s]

Now, at dusk, the curtains come down. Keepers took them down in the evening, so that nothing would stand between the lamp and the water. {c25~}

[pause 3s]

You fold them, one at a time. The linen is cold. The light behind it is grey and blue.

[pause 3s]

There is another rule here. While the light is burning, everything which does not belong in the lantern must be removed from it. {c26}

[pause 2s]

So you carry the curtains down a few steps, and leave them there, folded. {r42} The lantern holds only what it needs. The lens. The lamp. And you.

[pause 4s]

## 9. Линза

Пояснение: линза Френеля четвёртого порядка, неподвижный белый огонь — свет не вращается и не мигает.

[scene: close view of the lens; glass rings and prisms; the last daylight inside them]

Let us look at the lens for a moment, while there is still a little daylight in it.

[pause 2s]

It is a Fresnel lens, of the fourth order. The orders are sizes, and the fourth is one of the smaller ones. {c3} {c41}

[pause 3s]

It is built of rings of glass prisms, one above another, held in a metal frame. The prisms bend and reflect the light of one small flame, rays that would scatter in every direction, and send them out toward the water. {c41}

[pause 3s]

At this station, the light does not turn and does not flash. It is a fixed white light. It simply shines, steady, all night. {c2}

[pause 3s]

A ship coming up the bay will see it as a single, still point of white, marking the way into the river. {c1} {c2}

[pause 4s]

## 10. Зажечь лампу

Пояснение: лампу ставят внутрь линзы и только потом зажигают. Первые полчаса пламя держат низким, чтобы стекло лампы прогревалось медленно.

[scene: you place the lamp inside the lens; a match; a small low flame; the lens fills with a soft glow]

The sun is down now. The water has gone dark, and the sky over the far shore is the color of ashes. {c30}

[pause 3s]

The book says the lamp must be placed inside the lens, in its place, and then lighted. {c17}

[pause 2s]

So you set it down, gently, at the very center of all that glass.

[pause 3s]

You strike a match. The wick takes the flame.

[pause 4s]

And now comes the part that cannot be hurried.

[pause 2s]

For the first half hour, the flame must be kept low. The glass chimney of the lamp has to warm slowly. If you turn the wick up too soon, before the chimney and the burner are warm, the lamp will smoke. {c17}

[pause 4s]

So you keep it low. A small flame, in the middle of the great glass.

[pause 3s]

And slowly, the lens begins to fill. Ring after ring takes the light, until the whole lantern glows with it.

[pause 5s]

## 11. Свет над водой

Пояснение: огонь горит до восхода. Восход луны в 19:01 — после конца главы. Погода по карте Бюро погоды — для Истпорта, у маяка — реконструкция.

[scene: wide shot; the lit lantern; a flat band of light over the water; first stars; the empty hotel dark among the trees]

From the gallery outside, if you stepped out into the cold, you would see the light laid out over the water, low and level, toward the ships.

[pause 3s]

It will burn now until sunrise, at full strength, without a break. {c13}

[pause 3s]

The Weather Bureau's map for this evening shows clearing weather moving east, toward New England, and the forecast for tomorrow is generally fair. At Eastport, further along the coast, the thermometer will read ten degrees above zero at eight o'clock. {c32}

[pause 2s]

Here at the point, we can imagine the same: a cold, still, clear night. {r34}

[pause 3s]

The stars are coming out over the bay. And in about two and a half hours, a moon just past full will rise in the east, and lay its own pale road across the water. {c31}

[pause 4s]

The list of lights for these years gives no winter pause for this station. The rules say a light must always be shown if it is at all possible for vessels to benefit by it. {c33~}

[pause 4s]

## 12. Ночь впереди

Пояснение: правила на ночь. Без помощника смотритель дважды поднимается к свету между 8 вечера и восходом. Журнал: одна строка в день через два листа, с погодой.

[scene: back down the stairs; the workroom; the journal open on a small desk; a pen; the inkwell]

The lamp is lit. The hardest part of the evening is already done.

[pause 3s]

The government register for this year lists one name at this station, the keeper's. No assistant is listed. {c11}

[pause 2s]

For a keeper alone, the book says, the light must be visited at least twice during the night, between eight o'clock and sunrise. And on stormy nights, it must be constantly looked after. {c14}

[pause 3s]

Tonight is not a stormy night. So you will climb these stairs twice more, in the dark hours. That is all.

[pause 3s]

The station must never be left wholly unattended. If a keeper must be away, a member of his family, or another responsible person, must stay. {c15}

[pause 3s]

Later this evening, you will open the journal. {r43} Each day takes one line, written across both pages, in ink, neatly. The weather goes in it, and anything of interest on the water. {c27}

[pause 3s]

One line for a whole day. It seems very little. But on a quiet day, one line is enough.

[pause 3s]

Tomorrow is Sunday. The rules allow a keeper to be away on Sundays to attend church, so long as the light is never left without someone to watch it. {c43~} {c15}

[pause 3s]

But that is tomorrow. Tonight there is only tonight.

[pause 4s]

## 13. Конец главы

Пояснение: тихий выход. Без вопросов и обещаний «что будет дальше». Затемнение.

[scene: the camera rises slowly; the house window and the lantern, the two warm lights; the frame darkens]

So the first part of the night is done.

[pause 3s]

The lamp is burning low and steady, warming its glass. The kitchen is warm below. The bay is dark and calm.

[pause 3s]

There is nothing you need to do now. Nothing you need to remember.

[pause 3s]

Just the light above you, keeping its watch, so that you do not have to.

[pause 5s]

Rest now.

[pause 8s]
