# Option A: «Японский плакат одного предмета»

Read common.md (same folder) first: it holds the content, language, pictures and page contents.

Direction: the single-object poster that dominates the board. Board pins 07 KOREA, 09 PLAYSTATION, 14 VENUS, 19 POTHOS, 30 ANTHURIUM, 29 SUNSET JOURNEY, 34 CACTUS.

- Huge condensed headline across the full width at the top (the device name), the object (patent drawing) in the centre, thin columns of facts and data down the sides, small vertical text set like Japanese posters, small ornaments, thin rules, a "poster number" and date block at the bottom.
- Cream or warm paper with grain, one strong accent colour per episode (red, orange or green) plus dark ink. Calm, dense, collectible.
- Decorative Japanese is allowed only as correct Japanese for the content (for example フロッピーディスク = floppy disk, 磁気記録ディスク = magnetic record disk). No fake text.
- Fonts: a condensed display face (Anton, Bebas Neue, Big Shoulders Display or similar) and a small mono or grotesque for data; Noto Sans JP or Noto Serif JP for the Japanese.
