8 INCH / 1971

The plastic sleeve that made data portable.

Work began at IBM in San Jose in 1967. In 1971, the eight-inch disk shipped in the IBM 23FD drive: read-only, with a capacity of 80 KB.

Ralph Flores and Herbert E. Thompson filed “Magnetic record disk cover” on 22 December 1969. Assigned to IBM, the patent was granted on 6 June 1972.
