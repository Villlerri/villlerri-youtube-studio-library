# Option C: «Коллаж и гравюра»

Read common.md (same folder) first: it holds the content, language, pictures and page contents.

Direction: archival cut-paper collage. Board pins 33 (Hedy Lamarr patent collage: patent sheet as background, cut-out photo, black icon shapes on top), 26 (Marie Curie engraving with hand-lettered quote in a white blob), 35 "1984" (dense etched illustration, red title), 25 "FRAGILE" (crumpled object, starburst, sticker label), 22 RADIOHEAD (duotone print, stencil-like letters).

- Layers of paper: the full patent sheet as background, the drawing or the photo cut out with torn or scissor edges, tape strips, paper shadows, stamps and handwritten-style annotations pointing at parts.
- Mostly black and off-white like a photocopy or engraving, one accent (red or acid yellow-green) for titles and callouts. Heavy grain and halftone.
- The quote "rugged, inexpensive and mailable" set as hand lettering in a white shape, like pin 26.
- Fonts: a hand-lettered or marker display (for example Permanent Marker, Rubik Dirt, or Bangers) or a stencil, plus a typewriter face (Special Elite, Courier Prime) for annotations.
