Read common.md (same folder) first: it holds the content, language, pictures and page contents.

Direction: the device is presented as it was sold: a 1950s–60s print advertisement. Board pins 37 "Photography is an art." (flat black silhouette, orange shape, bold serif), 02 and 15 "Sit like a Lady" (big script and display lettering), 36 Kit-Kat 1935 (script logo over product, big year), 21 TWIST (bold headline wrapped around the object), 10 "Find the idea" (lamp, flat two-colour shapes), 17 "But first, coffee" (huge distressed serif).

- Flat two- or three-colour shapes drawn in CSS/SVG (hand holding the disk as a flat silhouette, a big circle, a beam), a confident ad headline, a script accent word, a price-tag or badge, small "fine print" columns.
- Colours: warm cream, black, one loud warm accent (orange or red), maybe a muted green. Slight print misregistration and worn ink texture.
- The patent drawing still appears (for example as an inset "as patented" panel or behind the headline), so the channel's idea stays visible.
- Fonts: a bold display serif or script pairing from Google Fonts (for example Abril Fatface, DM Serif Display, Yellowtail or Pacifico for the script word) plus a clean grotesque for small text.
