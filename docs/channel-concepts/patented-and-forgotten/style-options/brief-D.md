# Option D: «Тёмный журнал»

Read common.md (same folder) first: it holds the content, language, pictures and page contents.

Direction: dark editorial magazine page. Board pins 38/39 KIWI (dark olive page, title set vertically, thin frame line, long text column, object large and cut by the edge), 23 JAPAN (dark navy, thin frame, vertical title, red sun, small data line with coordinates, "Poster No. 001/182", barcode), 13 PULP FICTION (flat red-black contrast).

- Dark background (deep olive, ink navy or near-black), light cream text, thin hairline frames and rules, vertical or rotated title, long justified text columns from real facts, the drawing inverted to light lines and very large, cropped by the frame edge.
- One accent colour (signal red or kiwi green), used sparingly. Film grain and slight vignette from print, not glow.
- Small data lines: patent number, dates, "File No. 001", a CSS-drawn barcode.
- Fonts: a high-contrast condensed serif or a tall grotesque for the title (for example Bodoni Moda, Playfair Display SC, or Oswald) plus a light grotesque for body (for example Inter Tight or Archivo).
