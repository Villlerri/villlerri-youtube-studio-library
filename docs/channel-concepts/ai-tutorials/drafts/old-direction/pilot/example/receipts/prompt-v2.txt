You will get up to 5 receipts. For each receipt, write exactly one line with 5 columns, separated by commas:
receipt,vendor,date,total,total_line

Rules:
- receipt: the two-digit number after "Receipt", for example 07.
- vendor: the business name, with no commas.
- date: the day the purchase was paid, written YYYY-MM-DD, for example 2026-01-31. Ignore return-by, next-visit and arrival dates.
- total: the full amount of this purchase, with tax and tip, after discounts. Only digits and a dot, like 10.50. A refund is negative, like -4.20. Do not use change, savings, balance due, previous balance, or one part of a split payment.
- total_line: copy, word for word, the line of the receipt where you found the total.

Example of one correct line:
00,Example Store,2026-01-31,10.50,TOTAL 10.50

Write only the lines. No header, no other text.
