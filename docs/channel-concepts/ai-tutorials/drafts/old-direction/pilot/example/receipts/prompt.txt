You will get up to 5 receipts. For each receipt, write one line of CSV with these columns:
receipt,vendor,date,total,total_line

Rules:
- receipt: the two-digit number after "Receipt".
- vendor: the business name.
- date: the day the purchase was paid, written YYYY-MM-DD. Ignore return-by, next-visit and arrival dates.
- total: the full amount of this purchase, with tax and tip, after discounts. A refund is negative. Do not use change, savings, balance due, previous balance, or one part of a split payment.
- total_line: copy, word for word, the line of the receipt where you found the total.

Write only the CSV lines. No header, no other text.
